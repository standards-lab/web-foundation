## Task brief · components-settings

**Problem**

web-foundation v0.4.0 removed its theme menu and keys help, and now points pages to web-components
v0.2.0's sl-settings and sl-shortcuts, which don't exist yet. web-components is held at
foundation v0.3.0. It still reads the old color hooks, keeps its own copies of patterns the
foundation now ships, and has no icons layer. webappgen's `template` task needs v0.2.0 before it
can resume.

**Behaviors**
1. sl-settings, from spike-html-hosting-components v0.5.1:
   - It shows the color, scheme, space, and type rows. Each row is a ◀ value ▶ radio group, with
     H/L stepping within a row and J/K and the arrows moving between rows, beside an inert
     preview card.
   - It shows the character-key switch and the wait between key presses.
   - Every change is applied to the root and stored at once, through the foundation's
     preferences and keys modules. There is no Save and no Cancel.
   - Each change fires a bubbling, composed `change` event at the host.
   - It reads both settings again when its dialog opens, on a document `change`, and on another
     tab's `storage` event.
   - Its theme layout is the v0.3.0 theme-menu rules, moved into its own sheet.
2. sl-shortcuts lists, as a plain-text Keys | Action table:
   - exactly what `bindings(dialog)` returns, read again when the dialog opens and on each
     settings change;
   - "off" after each binding the reader's setting turns off;
   - the screen-reader note.

   Nothing in the table takes focus, and no binding runs.
3. sl-drawer's Close is a glyph button showing web-icons' `x-16`. Its visually hidden name
   appears as a hint on hover and on keyboard focus.
4. A boolean `modal` attribute, reflected, makes sl-drawer a modal dialog at every width, ignoring
   `dock`. Changing `modal` while the drawer is open closes it silently, as crossing `dock` does.
5. The package's peers and their pins:
   - web-foundation stays a peer, now at `^0.4.0`.
   - web-icons becomes a peer at `^0.1.0`, so the app's `iconFill()` plugin and the components
     resolve one copy.
   - Development installs each peer at its release tag.
   - The currency report, the release text, and the release test cover both peers.
6. The v0.3.0 hold is gone, and currency reports nothing held.
7. The components read `--color-accent` and `--color-primary`, with fallbacks. The old hook names
   appear nowhere.
8. The components adopt the foundation's `utilities.css`, `glyph-button.css`, `dialog.css`, and
   `motion.css` after reset and elements. No local utility, motion, field, or progress rule
   remains.
9. `json.js` is no longer shipped, and nothing re-exports it. Its consumers use `formatJson` and
   `tryFormatJson` from the foundation's `format.js`.
10. The layer-tree test names webappgen outright as the layer above.
11. The README and the demo page show the shortcut dialog pattern:
    - the page binds S and ?;
    - the openers are glyph buttons;
    - each dialog has a `.dialog-header` with a Close showing the Esc hint;
    - Help uses `.dialog-fit`, and Settings uses `.dialog-wide`.
12. Specimen captures of sl-settings, sl-shortcuts, and the drawer's Close and `modal` mode, in
    light and dark and in every theme, for the architect's design review.
