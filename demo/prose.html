A reading page

The reading column is 80ch at most, centered between the page's gutters, which are --page-gutter, the space profile's --space-m. At a phone's width it is the viewport less the gutters, and at twice the text size the header's buttons wrap under the page's name rather than scroll the page sideways.

Sections

Each section opens on its heading, --space-xl below what came before it. The elements layer spaces the blocks inside a section, and the heading sits close to what it names.

A second paragraph follows the first a block's space below it, as the elements layer sets any block after a block.

The end of the page

Main ends --space-2xl below its last line, so the page's end isn't flush with the viewport's foot when the reader scrolls to it.
